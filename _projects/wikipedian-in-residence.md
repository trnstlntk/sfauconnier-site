I've twice worked as a Wikipedian (or Wikimedian) in Residence: a role that embeds someone with Wikimedia expertise inside a cultural or academic institution, to help staff contribute their collections and knowledge to Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons and Wikidata directly, and to build up the skills to keep doing so independently afterwards.

## Expedition Wikipedia — Stichting Academisch Erfgoed (2014–2015)

From January 2014, I was Wikipedian in Residence for Stichting Academisch Erfgoed, working on the project "Expeditie Wikipedia." Several Dutch universities and the Tropenmuseum, united in the foundation, made digitized materials from (historic) scientific expeditions accessible to a broad public via Wikipedia. I supported the participating institutions, organized activities to stimulate the use of their collections on Wikipedia, and took care of uploading their media to Wikimedia Commons.

## Memory of the World — UNESCO Netherlands (2017)

In spring and summer 2017, I was Wikipedian in Residence for a project connecting Dutch UNESCO Memory of the World heritage with Wikimedia. Guided by UNESCO, Netwerk Digitaal Erfgoed and Wikimedia Nederland, I worked with three institutions — Bijzondere Collecties (Utrecht University Library), Zeeuws Archief, and the International Institute of Social History — training staff on Wikipedia, Commons, Wikidata and Wikisource, and advising on digital access and copyright, so they could continue this work on their own afterwards.
