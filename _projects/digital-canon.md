In 2018, I was part of the working group that compiled the Digital Canon of the Netherlands, commissioned by LIMA (Living Media Art) and supported by the Creative Industries Fund NL. Together with Josephine Bosma, Martijn van Boven, Annet Dekker, and Jan Robert Leegte, we selected twenty pioneering digital artworks made on Dutch soil, created between the 1960s and 2000.

The goal wasn't to create a fixed, definitive ranking, but to bring visibility to important digital art that risked being overlooked or forgotten, and to spark discussion about how — and whether — a canon for digital art should exist at all. We discussed this at length in a working session titled "Canonisation as an Activist Act," together with Gaby Wijers (LIMA). The Digital Canon website launched in 2019, with all twenty works and their documentation.

In 2023–2024, those twenty works formed the core of *REBOOT. Pioneering Digital Art*, an exhibition at Het Nieuwe Instituut in Rotterdam (7 October 2023 – 12 May 2024), curated by Sanneke Huisman (LI-MA) and Klaas Kuitenbrouwer (Het Nieuwe Instituut). Alongside the canonical works, the exhibition presented nine new commissions by contemporary artists responding to them.
