I made my very first edit on the English Wikipedia on 1 September 2003 — I created (!) the article on Magnum Photos. Since then, I've stayed active on the English Wikipedia, made some edits on the Dutch Wikipedia, and contributed to Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons.

I'm not the most fanatic editor, but I'm pretty faithful to this project, which I see as a miraculous manifestation of human knowledge and goodwill — one of the best things the internet has brought forward, a resource that belongs to us all and that we all share a responsibility for.

I write about topics I'm passionate about — art, culture, design, architecture — that still need quite a bit of attention. In 2012, I was a board member for cultural projects at Wikimedia NL, and I still volunteer in person every now and then. I do this for free — it's fun, very fulfilling and rewarding, and Wikipedians are amazing people.
