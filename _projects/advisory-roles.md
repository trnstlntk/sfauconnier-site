Advisory panel member, listed as "art historian and Wikimedian."

### LINCS: Linked Infrastructure for Networked Cultural Scholarship — Technical Advisory Board (dates to confirm)

Technical Advisory Board member for LINCS, a Canadian project converting and interlinking research datasets on cultural heritage as Linked Open Data.

* [LINCS committees and boards page](https://lincsproject.ca/docs/about-lincs/people/committees)

### OpenRefine — Steering Committee member (2020–present)

Member of OpenRefine's Steering Committee since January 2020, initially representing the Wikimedia Foundation. From February to December 2022, I additionally took on the part-time role of Project Director, before handing my responsibilities back to the advisory committee.

* [OpenRefine joins Code for Science and Society (2020)](https://openrefine.org/blog/2020/01/31/css)
* [OpenRefine welcomes Sandra Fauconnier as project director (2022)](https://openrefine.org/blog/2022/03/03/project-director)
* [Governance changes (2022)](https://openrefine.org/blog/2022/12/01/governance-changes)

Member of the independent advisory committee for the Fund's E-Culture grant programme (Stimuleringsfonds Creatieve Industrie).

* [Creative Industries Fund NL](https://stimuleringsfonds.nl/)
